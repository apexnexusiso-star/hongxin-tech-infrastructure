Misunderstandings to Remove

Remove this misunderstanding: The SoA is a catalogue. The SoA should explain applicability, selection, exclusion and status in relation to risks, impacts and scope.

Remove this misunderstanding: Residual risk is a technical footnote. Residual risk acceptance is a management decision that should name authority and rationale.

Remove this misunderstanding: Review happens only on a calendar. Supplier change, drift, complaints, new use and regulatory change can all trigger review outside the annual cycle.
