Misunderstandings to Remove

Remove this misunderstanding: Audit programme is scheduling. The programme should consider risks, objectives, changes, prior results, resources and competence.

Remove this misunderstanding: Stage 1 is paperwork only. Stage 1 checks readiness, scope, context, inventory and whether Stage 2 can be planned defensibly.

Remove this misunderstanding: Sampling waits until onsite work. Sampling logic begins during preparation because it shapes team competence, time and evidence requests.
