Learning Check: Keep the Audit Boundary

Pause after Slide 09 and choose wording that stays evidence-based.

Which conclusion style is strongest for a training audit discussion?

Option 1: Bounded wording. For the sampled VisionLine QC process, leadership responsibilities were assigned on paper but the organization did not demonstrate authority for delaying production when AI inspection controls were weakened.
Option 2: Broad judgement. The AI system is unsafe and the organization is not ready for certification.
Option 3: No issue. No issue exists because the organization gives a comfortable explanation.

Coaching note: Good finding language is narrow enough to be fair and specific enough to be useful. The hidden audit issue here is: A named executive and signed policy do not prove that business managers, production owners and quality leaders follow governance when delivery pressure conflicts with AI controls.

This is a learning pause, not a formal examination. Use it to test the direction of your audit reasoning before moving on.
