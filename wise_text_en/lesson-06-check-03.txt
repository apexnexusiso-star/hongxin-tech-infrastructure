Learning Check: Keep the Audit Boundary

Pause after Slide 09 and choose wording that stays evidence-based.

Which conclusion style is strongest for a training audit discussion?

Option 1: Bounded wording. For the sampled TutorPath EU deployment, the organization had not linked applicable AI obligations and effective dates to scope, transparency preparation, supplier monitoring and review triggers.
Option 2: Broad judgement. The AI system is unsafe and the organization is not ready for certification.
Option 3: No issue. No issue exists because the organization gives a comfortable explanation.

Coaching note: Good finding language is narrow enough to be fair and specific enough to be useful. The hidden audit issue here is: AIMS scope and planning need to know which obligations affect which AI uses and when controls, transparency duties, governance duties or high-risk rules become relevant.

This is a learning pause, not a formal examination. Use it to test the direction of your audit reasoning before moving on.
