Misunderstandings to Remove

Remove this misunderstanding: Policy equals leadership. Policy matters when it influences objectives, resource decisions, escalation and review.

Remove this misunderstanding: Responsibility can sit in one AI office. AI governance usually crosses business, technology, data, risk, legal and operational ownership.

Remove this misunderstanding: Commercial pressure is outside the audit. Pressure tests whether leadership commitment operates when controls become inconvenient.
