Learning Check: Keep the Audit Boundary

Pause after Slide 09 and choose wording that stays evidence-based.

Which conclusion style is strongest for a training audit discussion?

Option 1: Bounded wording. For the sampled EssayMark scoring period, the organization did not demonstrate that repeated teacher complaints triggered impact-assessment review and corrective action for feedback affecting English-language learners.
Option 2: Broad judgement. The AI system is unsafe and the organization is not ready for certification.
Option 3: No issue. No issue exists because the organization gives a comfortable explanation.

Coaching note: Good finding language is narrow enough to be fair and specific enough to be useful. The hidden audit issue here is: Immediate correction may be useful, but the audit still has to decide whether impact assessment, monitoring, teacher review or corrective action processes failed.

This is a learning pause, not a formal examination. Use it to test the direction of your audit reasoning before moving on.
