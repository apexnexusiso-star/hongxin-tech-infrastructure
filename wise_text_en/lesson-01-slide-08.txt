Finding Language and Professional Boundary

Professional boundary matters. The weak report phrase would be: The organization has an AI ethics statement, so AI governance is addressed. That sentence may be tempting because it sounds decisive, but it reaches beyond what many audits can prove.

A stronger audit phrase for BrightReply Support AI is: For the sampled BrightReply use, the organization has not shown how AI-assisted replies are identified, reviewed and fed into risk review when customer commitments are affected.
