Finding Language and Professional Boundary

Professional boundary matters. The weak report phrase would be: The SoA is complete because every control family has an implementation status. That sentence may be tempting because it sounds decisive, but it reaches beyond what many audits can prove.

A stronger audit phrase for LimitWise Credit Advisor is: For the sampled LimitWise update, the SoA and residual-risk acceptance were not reviewed after supplier model changes that affected credit-limit recommendations and manual overrides. This wording names the sample, the management-system weakness and the reason it matters without turning Samira O'Neill into the system designer, regulator or sector specialist.

When learners practice with LimitWise Credit Advisor, ask them to underline the criterion in their sentence, circle the sampled use from one model update release and a sampled limit-increase campaign for young customers, and mark the evidence gap. If those parts are missing, the finding is still classroom draft material.
