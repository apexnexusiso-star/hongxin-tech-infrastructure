In BrightDesk Services - BrightReply Support AI, which evidence would you sample first to test real-use responsibility trace?

Option 1: Sample path. Start from five closed complaint tickets where the agent used the assistant and the customer later challenged the answer, then connect it to AI use inventory, ticket audit trail, agent training scenario.

Option 3: Supplier only. Ask the supplier or technical team to confirm that the AI system is acceptable.

Coaching note: A good answer forms one chain: sample, affected use, evidence object, responsible role, decision record and improvement trigger. In this lesson, the memory hook is real-use responsibility trace.
