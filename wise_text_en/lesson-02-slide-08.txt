Misunderstandings to Remove

Remove this misunderstanding: 42001 certifies that every model is good. The standard addresses the management system. It does not turn the auditor into a model validator.

Remove this misunderstanding: Technical experts replace auditors. Experts support technical understanding, while the auditor still owns audit criteria, evidence sufficiency and findings.

Remove this misunderstanding: One authorization covers all AI audits. Authorization should consider role, AI use, sector risk, witnessed performance and boundaries of judgement.
