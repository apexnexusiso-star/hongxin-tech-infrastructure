Learning Check: Keep the Audit Boundary

Pause after Slide 09 and choose wording that stays evidence-based.

Which conclusion style is strongest for a training audit discussion?

Option 1: Bounded wording. For the sampled BrightReply use, the organization has not shown how AI-assisted replies are identified, reviewed and fed into risk review when customer commitments are affected.
Option 2: Broad judgement. The AI system is unsafe and the organization is not ready for certification.
Option 3: No issue. No issue exists because the organization gives a comfortable explanation.

Coaching note: Good finding language is narrow enough to be fair and specific enough to be useful. The hidden audit issue here is: The AI draft has become part of the service process. It influences customer commitments, complaint handling and refund decisions even when a human clicks the final button.

This is a learning pause, not a formal examination. Use it to test the direction of your audit reasoning before moving on.
