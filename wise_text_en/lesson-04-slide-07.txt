Misunderstandings to Remove

Remove this misunderstanding: Evidence is any document with the right title. Evidence must be relevant to the criterion and the conclusion, not merely related to the topic.

Remove this misunderstanding: One strong technical report closes the audit question. Technical reports often need to be connected to approval, use, monitoring and exception handling.

Remove this misunderstanding: The auditor should investigate until every fact is known. Sampling seeks enough appropriate evidence for a conclusion, not total reconstruction of the business event.
