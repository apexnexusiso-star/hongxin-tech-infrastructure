Learning Check: Build the Evidence Chain

Pause after Slide 06 and connect records instead of listing them.

Which evidence route is most useful for this point in the lesson?

Option 1: Connected chain. Connect intended-use statement, requirements trace, validation report with interviews and the sampled decision or review record.
Option 2: One record. Check whether Intended-Use Statement exists and stop there.
Option 3: Verbal assurance. Rely on the process owner's explanation if the answer sounds mature and consistent.

Coaching note: The practical move is to connect records, roles and decisions. In this case, start with one pre-launch pilot for graduate hiring where recruiters receive ranking bands and summary reasons, then test whether intended-use statement, requirements trace, validation report support each other.

This is a learning pause, not a formal examination. Use it to test the direction of your audit reasoning before moving on.
