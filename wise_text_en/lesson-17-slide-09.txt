Misunderstandings to Remove

Remove this misunderstanding: Interview confidence equals evidence. Interviews are valuable, but they usually need corroboration through records or demonstrations.

Remove this misunderstanding: Technical complexity means the auditor must accept the expert's word. The auditor can use experts while still requiring traceable evidence and clear conclusions.

Remove this misunderstanding: Logs always settle the issue. Logs need interpretation, scope, timestamps, completeness and connection to the sampled decision.
