Opening Dialogue

Instructor: Hello everyone, and welcome back to Lesson 4 of the ISO/IEC 42001 Audit Competence course.

Learner: If a procedure exists and a record exists, is that enough to call the requirement implemented?

Instructor: Not necessarily. The auditor has to connect requirement, implementation, evidence and judgement. A record can be impressive and still be insufficient for the conclusion being drawn.

We are continuing from Lesson 3, and today the audit lens moves into a new part of the AIMS. Our focus is evidence sufficiency chain. The lesson is not a reading of the textbook; it is a guided way to turn the textbook into audit movement.

By the end, you should be able to translate ISO/IEC 42001 requirements into audit criteria and evidence needs; judge whether evidence is relevant, current and sufficient; avoid confusing document existence with implementation effectiveness; and write conclusions that stay within the sampled evidence.
