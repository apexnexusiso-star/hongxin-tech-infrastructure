Misunderstandings to Remove

Remove this misunderstanding: Lifecycle audit means checking a project plan. The plan matters, but the audit follows decisions, evidence and approvals across the lifecycle.

Remove this misunderstanding: Validation is the whole launch gate. Validation is one input; launch readiness also includes intended use, limitations, users, controls and residual decisions.

Remove this misunderstanding: Auditors approve the design. Auditors test whether the organization's lifecycle controls operated; they do not design the system for the auditee.
