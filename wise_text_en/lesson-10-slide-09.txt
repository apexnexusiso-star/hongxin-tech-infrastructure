Misunderstandings to Remove

Remove this misunderstanding: Resources mean only budget. Resources include people, tools, data access, monitoring capability, time and external support needed for AIMS operation.

Remove this misunderstanding: Awareness equals competence. Awareness tells people that rules exist; competence shows they can perform role-specific tasks.

Remove this misunderstanding: Document control is administrative. In AI use, obsolete prompts, guidance or knowledge articles can become live risk sources.
