Misunderstandings to Remove

Remove this misunderstanding: Operation is maintenance only. Operation includes monitoring, issue detection, change control, incident handling, user behavior and improvement.

Remove this misunderstanding: One performance metric is enough. AI monitoring may need segmentation by line, group, data source, condition or use case.

Remove this misunderstanding: Retirement removes responsibility. Archived outputs, customers affected by earlier decisions and knowledge bases may create residual obligations.
