Misunderstandings to Remove

Remove this misunderstanding: Competence is knowledge only. Competence includes knowledge, skill, judgement, behavior, experience and maintained performance evidence.

Remove this misunderstanding: Technical experts can lead the audit by default. Technical experts may support interpretation, but audit leadership requires management-system audit competence.

Remove this misunderstanding: Authorization never narrows. Authorization can be staged by role, sector, system type, risk level and need for technical support.
