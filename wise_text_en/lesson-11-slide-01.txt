Opening Dialogue

Instructor: Hello everyone, and welcome back to Lesson 11 of the ISO/IEC 42001 Audit Competence course.

Learner: If a project team writes that the AI system will be fair and transparent, does that settle the launch-readiness question?

Instructor: No. Launch readiness needs evidence from intended use, requirements, design, data, validation, approval, user instruction and control of remaining limitations.

We are continuing from Lesson 10, and today the audit lens moves into a new part of the AIMS. Our focus is launch gate walk-through. The lesson is not a reading of the textbook; it is a guided way to turn the textbook into audit movement.

By the end, you should be able to audit lifecycle stages before deployment; connect intended use with requirements, validation and launch approval; test design changes that alter AI impact before release; and evaluate launch readiness without becoming the designer.
