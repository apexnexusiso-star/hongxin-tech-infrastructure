Finding Language and Professional Boundary

Professional boundary matters. The weak report phrase would be: A general ISO auditor can audit any AI system if the checklist is complete. That sentence may be tempting because it sounds decisive, but it reaches beyond what many audits can prove.

A stronger audit phrase for AIMS Auditor Authorization Panel is: For the sampled audit assignment, the certification body did not demonstrate that the audit team competence matched the AI systems, impact pathways and technical boundaries in the client scope.
