In CrediBridge Finance - LimitWise Credit Advisor, which evidence would you sample first to test SoA reality test?

Option 1: Sample path. Start from one model update release and a sampled limit-increase campaign for young customers, then connect it to risk treatment plan, SoA rationale, model update notice.

Option 3: Supplier only. Ask the supplier or technical team to confirm that the AI system is acceptable.

Coaching note: A good answer forms one chain: sample, affected use, evidence object, responsible role, decision record and improvement trigger. In this lesson, the memory hook is SoA reality test.
