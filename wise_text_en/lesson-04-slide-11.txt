In NovaHire Technologies - NovaHire Rank, which evidence would you sample first to test evidence sufficiency chain?

Option 1: Sample path. Start from one recruitment campaign where the top-ranked candidates were accepted without the documented human-review step, then connect it to procedure revision history, validation report, campaign ranking export.

Coaching note: A good answer forms one chain: sample, affected use, evidence object, responsible role, decision record and improvement trigger. In this lesson, the memory hook is evidence sufficiency chain.
