Learning Check: Build the Evidence Chain

Pause after Slide 06 and connect records instead of listing them.

Which evidence route is most useful for this point in the lesson?

Option 1: Connected chain. Connect complaint log, impact assessment, monitoring report with interviews and the sampled decision or review record.
Option 2: One record. Check whether Complaint Log exists and stop there.
Option 3: Verbal assurance. Rely on the process owner's explanation if the answer sounds mature and consistent.

Coaching note: The practical move is to connect records, roles and decisions. In this case, start with one scoring period where feedback templates were changed manually after teacher complaints, then test whether complaint log, impact assessment, monitoring report support each other.

This is a learning pause, not a formal examination. Use it to test the direction of your audit reasoning before moving on.
