Misunderstandings to Remove

Remove this misunderstanding: Role labels are legal decoration. Role labels guide evidence routes and responsibility boundaries in the management system.

Remove this misunderstanding: Interested parties mean only direct customers. Applicants, branch staff, regulators, supplier personnel and complaint handlers may all shape needs and expectations.

Remove this misunderstanding: Scope is whatever the organization writes. Scope must be consistent with actual AI use, role boundaries, context and applicable obligations.
