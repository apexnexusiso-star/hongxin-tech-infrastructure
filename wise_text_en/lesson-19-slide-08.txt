Misunderstandings to Remove

Remove this misunderstanding: Consistency means identical sampling. Consistency means applying the same audit principles while adapting evidence to context.

Remove this misunderstanding: Cases are classroom decoration. Cases train judgement: what to sample, who to interview, what evidence conflicts matter and how findings differ.

Remove this misunderstanding: Sector expertise replaces ISO logic. Sector expertise informs interpretation; ISO/IEC 42001 still provides the management-system audit frame.
