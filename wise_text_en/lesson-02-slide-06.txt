Let us make the lesson concrete. The organization is Northbridge Certification Services, working in certification body competence management. The sampled system is AIMS Auditor Authorization Panel. Then comes the comfortable but dangerous answer: Our ISO 27001 lead auditors understand controls and risk, so they can be authorized for every 42001 audit immediately. It sounds efficient and reassuring. It also hides the weakness that the lesson is designed to expose.

The hidden issue is this: Information security competence helps, but it does not automatically cover AI impact assessment, model change, data drift, human oversight or third-party AI service evidence. For AIMS Auditor Authorization Panel, none of these records is magic by itself. Their value appears when they fit together and explain what happened in the sample.

In Northbridge Certification Services, comfort and evidence pull in different directions. The comfortable answer lowers the pressure in the room.
