Audit Forward and Audit Backward

Use audit forward from addition of a hospital pilot before certification audit planning was finalized. For MetroCare Health Network, follow that change into scope, then into the risk or impact record, then into the selected control for CareRoute AI Portfolio. Only after that should the route reach operating instruction, monitoring and management review.

Use audit backward from a Stage 2 audit plan that samples only central office documents. Start with the record, complaint, log or outcome in one hospital pilot of an appointment triage assistant missing from the central AI inventory, then walk back to the requirement that should have governed it. This movement keeps Grace from accepting a general explanation for a specific sample.

For CareRoute AI Portfolio, maintenance means MetroCare Health Network can show that documents exist and have been updated. For the one hospital pilot of an appointment triage assistant missing from the central AI inventory sample, effectiveness means those documents influenced the AI use when evidence became inconvenient for the auditee. That distinction keeps Lesson 16 practical.

Finding Language and Professional Boundary

Professional boundary matters. The weak report phrase would be: Stage 1 can rely on the auditee's declared scope without testing real AI use. That sentence may be tempting because it sounds decisive, but it reaches beyond what many audits can prove.

A stronger audit phrase for CareRoute AI Portfolio is: For the MetroCare Stage 1 review, the audit team identified that the declared scope and AI inventory did not support reliable Stage 2 sampling for multi-site AI use. This wording names the sample, the management-system weakness and the reason it matters without turning Grace Patel into the system designer, regulator or sector specialist.

When learners practice with CareRoute AI Portfolio, ask them to underline the criterion in their sentence, circle the sampled use from one hospital pilot of an appointment triage assistant missing from the central AI inventory, and mark the evidence gap. If those parts are missing, the finding is still classroom draft material.
