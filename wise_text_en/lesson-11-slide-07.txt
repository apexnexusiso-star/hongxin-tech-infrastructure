Use audit forward from a late design change adding a confidence band to the candidate ranking screen. Finding Language and Professional Boundary

Professional boundary matters. The weak report phrase would be: The launch gate is satisfied because validation metrics met the internal target. That sentence may be tempting because it sounds decisive, but it reaches beyond what many audits can prove.

A stronger audit phrase for CandidateFlow Screening is: For the sampled CandidateFlow launch, the organization did not demonstrate that the late confidence-band design change was assessed against intended use, recruiter behavior and human-review controls before release approval. This wording names the sample, the management-system weakness and the reason it matters without turning Oliver Grant into the system designer, regulator or sector specialist.
