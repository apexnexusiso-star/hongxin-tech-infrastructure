Misunderstandings to Remove

Remove this misunderstanding: A finding must sound dramatic. A professional finding is precise, evidence-based and proportionate.

Remove this misunderstanding: Correction cancels nonconformity. Correction fixes the immediate issue; corrective action addresses cause and prevents recurrence where a requirement failed.

Remove this misunderstanding: Report language should judge the AI system. The auditor reports management-system evidence and requirement failure, not unsupported technical or legal conclusions.
