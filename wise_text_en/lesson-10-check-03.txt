Learning Check: Keep the Audit Boundary

Pause after Slide 09 and choose wording that stays evidence-based.

Which conclusion style is strongest for a training audit discussion?

Option 1: Bounded wording. For the sampled CivicAnswer benefits inquiry, the organization could not show role-specific competence and current documented information for staff using AI-assisted public-service answers.
Option 2: Broad judgement. The AI system is unsafe and the organization is not ready for certification.
Option 3: No issue. No issue exists because the organization gives a comfortable explanation.

Coaching note: Good finding language is narrow enough to be fair and specific enough to be useful. The hidden audit issue here is: General awareness does not prove that staff can recognize high-risk citizen situations, use current guidance, escalate wrong answers or preserve records for public-service accountability.

This is a learning pause, not a formal examination. Use it to test the direction of your audit reasoning before moving on.
