Opening Dialogue

Instructor: Hello everyone, and welcome to Lesson 1 of the ISO/IEC 42001 Audit Competence course.

Learner: If AI tools are already everywhere, why do we need a management system before we can audit them?

Instructor: Because the tool is not the whole problem. The management question begins when AI output changes work, responsibility, customer expectation or affected-party impact.

This is the first lesson, so we begin by building the reason for the whole course. Our focus is real-use responsibility trace. The lesson is not a reading of the textbook; it is a guided way to turn the textbook into audit movement.

By the end, you should be able to explain why AI governance is a management-system problem, not only a technical review topic; connect AI use, risk, opportunity and responsibility in ordinary business language; identify the first evidence route an auditor should open before judging AIMS maturity; and avoid treating a general AI policy as proof that real AI use is governed.
