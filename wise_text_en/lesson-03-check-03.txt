Learning Check: Keep the Audit Boundary

Pause after Slide 09 and choose wording that stays evidence-based.

Which conclusion style is strongest for a training audit discussion?

Option 1: Bounded wording. For the sampled retail copilot change, the organization maintained a clause-aligned manual but did not demonstrate that the PDCA loop captured AI-assisted product claims, related interested-party needs and updated controls.
Option 2: Broad judgement. The AI system is unsafe and the organization is not ready for certification.
Option 3: No issue. No issue exists because the organization gives a comfortable explanation.

Coaching note: Good finding language is narrow enough to be fair and specific enough to be useful. The hidden audit issue here is: The clause shell may look complete, but the AIMS loop has not shown that it detects new AI uses, reassesses impact or updates controls when merchandising practice changes.

This is a learning pause, not a formal examination. Use it to test the direction of your audit reasoning before moving on.
