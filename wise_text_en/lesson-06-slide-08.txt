Misunderstandings to Remove

Remove this misunderstanding: AI Act equals ISO 42001. The EU AI Act is a legal regime; ISO/IEC 42001 is a management-system standard. They can interact, but they are not the same instrument.

Remove this misunderstanding: Only EU dates matter. The EU timeline is a useful teaching example, while US state rules, Chinese rules, UK guidance and sector regulations may matter for other scopes.

Remove this misunderstanding: A legal register is enough. The register must connect to AI uses, owners, effective dates, controls, communication and review triggers.
